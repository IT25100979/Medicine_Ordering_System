UPLOAD YOUR PRESCRIPTION

Our licensed pharmacists review and verify your doctor's order before direct-to-door fulfillment.

1

Ensure Patient & Doctor Information Is Clearly Visible

Full legal name, date of birth, prescriber's clinic name, DEA/NPI license number, and signature must be sharp and legible.

2

Verify Medication Details & Dosages

Double-check that the drug name, exact strength (e.g., mg, mcg), quantity, and specific intake frequency are uncropped.

3

Capture High-Resolution, Well-Lit Photos or PDFs

Place physical paper scripts on a flat dark surface with even lighting to avoid glare, shadows, or blurry text.

4

Check the Prescription Expiration Date

Most standard prescriptions must be dated within the past 12 months (or 6 months for regulated maintenance medications).

5

Upload All Pages If Multi-Sheet or Electronic E-Script

If your prescription includes supplementary dosage schedules, refills, or physician notes, attach all pages together.
